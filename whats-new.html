DNS 2027 · Unlocking Innovation

The Summit keeps growing.

Every year, the Davos Neurodiversity Summit becomes a little more than the week in Switzerland. 2027 is our most immersive year yet — a new global prize, six innovation tracks, a companion accessibility app, and expanded programming in Chicago — all part of a platform that now runs year-round, not just during WEF week.

New for 2027

The Neurodiversity Innovation Prize.

A new global award for a startup, founder, or neurodiverse team using neurodiverse insight to improve work, learning, health, accessibility, AI, design, or quality of life.

Prize Package

What the winner receives

  • The award itself, presented on the Davos stage
  • A featured profile on the DNS Leadership Wall
  • Media assets and storytelling support

Sponsor Package

What a naming partner receives

  • Naming rights to the Prize
  • A seat on the judging panel
  • Direct access to founders and pilot pathways

Practical Accessibility Innovation

The Inclusive Davos Experience.

A sensory-friendly app and map helping neurodivergent leaders navigate Davos with more confidence, comfort, and belonging during WEF week.

In the App

  • Quiet zones and recharge spaces
  • Real-time crowd level indicators
  • Low-sensory walking routes

Also Included

  • Curated WEF-adjacent event listings
  • Small-group gathering options
  • A replicable model other convenings can adopt
