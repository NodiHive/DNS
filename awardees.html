Honoring 2026, Looking Toward 2027

Seventeen leaders, one recognition.

Before we look ahead to DNS 2027, a look back at the leaders recognized in 2026 — across business, policy, education, culture, and community. Filter by the kind of impact that interests you.

How awardees are chosen

DNS recognizes that meaningful systems change requires many forms of leadership — business, research, policy, culture, institutional infrastructure, and lived experience. The awardees above reflect that range deliberately, not by accident.

Selections are based on nominations received and independent research; personal relationships, politics, and marketing considerations play no role in the process. Know someone who should be on our radar for 2027? Let us know at contact@davosneurodiversitysummit.com.
